We are a family-owned coffee and tea house that has been serving the community for over 20 years. Our mission is to provide our customers with the highest quality coffee and tea, along with a warm and welcoming atmosphere.

We source our coffee beans from the finest growers around the world, and we roast them in-house to ensure maximum freshness and flavor. Our tea selection includes a wide variety of loose-leaf teas, as well as herbal blends and specialty teas.

In addition to our coffee and tea offerings, we also serve a variety of pastries, sandwiches, and other light fare. We believe that food should be made with care and attention to detail, which is why we use only the freshest ingredients in our recipes.

At Aroma Coffee & Tea House, we are committed to sustainability and ethical sourcing practices. We work closely with our suppliers to ensure that our products are produced in an environmentally responsible manner, and we strive to minimize our impact on the planet.
