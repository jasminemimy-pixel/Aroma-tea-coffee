Our Story

Aroma Coffee & Tea House was founded in 2003 by a group of coffee enthusiasts who shared a passion for quality and community. What started as a small local business has grown into a beloved institution in our neighborhood.

Our journey began with a simple mission: to create a space where people could come together to enjoy exceptional coffee and tea while building meaningful connections. Over the years, we have remained true to this vision, continuously striving to improve our offerings and enhance the customer experience.

We take pride in our commitment to sustainability and ethical sourcing practices. We work closely with our suppliers to ensure that our products are produced in an environmentally responsible manner, and we strive to minimize our impact on the planet.

Our Vision

At Aroma Coffee & Tea House, we envision a world where every cup of coffee and tea is a moment of joy and connection. We aim to be a place where people can escape the hustle and bustle of daily life, relax, and savor the simple pleasures of life.

We are dedicated to fostering a sense of community and belonging, where everyone feels welcome and valued. Our vision is to create a space that inspires creativity, conversation, and collaboration, where ideas can flourish and friendships can blossom.

We are committed to continuous improvement and innovation, always seeking new ways to enhance our offerings and exceed our customers' expectations. Our vision is to be a leader in the coffee and tea industry, setting the standard for quality, sustainability, and customer service.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide our customers with the highest quality coffee and tea, along with a warm and welcoming atmosphere. We are dedicated to sourcing the finest ingredients, roasting our coffee beans in-house, and offering a diverse selection of teas to cater to every taste.

We strive to create a memorable experience for our customers, where they can enjoy exceptional beverages, delicious food, and a sense of community. Our mission is to be a place where people can come together, connect, and create lasting memories.

We are committed to sustainability and ethical sourcing practices, working closely with our suppliers to ensure that our products are produced in an environmentally responsible manner. Our mission is to make a positive impact on the world, one cup at a time.
